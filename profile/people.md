## How to ask for a letter

Send a packet:
1. The application's deadline + portal details + reviewer criteria URL.
2. Your current CV (use `Career/Resume & CV/McGowan_Douglas_CV.pdf`).
3. The relevant research/personal statement draft, even if rough.
4. `Ship Apps/Guide to Writing My Rec Letter.pdf` — the existing guide.
5. A one-line reminder of which specific accomplishments are most relevant for *this* fellowship's criteria.
6. Two weeks of lead time minimum. Four weeks for top-priority applications (Hertz, NDSEG).
