## Known discrepancies to reconcile before submission

1. **DM small-group count.** CV says "8 different teams"; Hertz long statement says "10 small groups." Reconcile in next CV revision — use **10** in current narrative documents.
2. **Learning Factory project completion rate.** CV says "250+ students each year"; some Hertz language says "200+." Prefer **250+** (the CV number is the most-recent and most-authoritative).
3. **NUS people-counting camera.** CV's "Product Design (Singapore)" entry calls the prototype a *people-counting camera to increase safety in work trucks*. Make sure the same description shows up consistently when this project is referenced.
4. **Boxing first-tryout date.** Hertz long statement implies year 1 = freshman/sophomore club training; the team-membership entry is **May 2024 – May 2025** (senior year). Be careful not to imply a multi-year team membership; it's one team year preceded by a longer training-club year.
5. **"Patent application" at Rivian.** The exact patent disclosure language should be cross-checked with the Rivian internal record before being repeated in a published bio.
6. **NASA family detail.** GRFP says both parents had decorated NASA engineering careers; another fragment notes a single-mother NASA-engineer parent. Both can be true (single mother who is a NASA engineer + father with NASA engineering career), but be specific when asked rather than generic.
