## 250-word

> Douglas McGowan is a Mechanical Engineering PhD student at UC Berkeley, advised by Prof. Kosa Goucher-Lambert in the Co-Design Lab and a recipient of the UC Berkeley Chancellor's Fellowship. His research develops a human–AI co-creation framework that integrates manufacturability into the earliest stages of CAD/CAM design — reducing the costly late-stage rework that drives the system development bottleneck. He held research roles at Penn State's Applied Research Lab — where he wrote his honors thesis on dynamic analysis of composite–steel bolted joints — and at Purdue's Zucrow Labs on a DARPA-sponsored propellant characterization project.
